Key Findings Summary

 Correlation Structure: 49 significant correlations identified among morphometric traits

 Dimensionality Reduction: First 3 PCs explain 78.5% of total variance

 Major Drivers: X.5, X.7 are the strongest contributors to PC1

Group Separation: Some overlap observed between groups

Statistical Interpretation

What the PCA tells us:

PC1 (51%): Represents the primary axis of morphometric variation. Variables with high loadings on PC1 (X.5, X.7, X.4) are the most important traits distinguishing individuals.

PC2 (17.2%): Captures independent variation orthogonal to PC1. This axis highlights different aspects of morphology that vary independently of the main pattern.

Group Differences: The positions of group centroids and the overlap (or separation) of confidence ellipses indicate whether groups differ significantly in their morphometric characteristics.
